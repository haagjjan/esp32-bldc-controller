# Hardware

Place editable schematics and PCB layouts here once the team chooses its CAD tool. Record the tool/version and board revision alongside each design.

Planned contents:

- `pcb/`: editable schematic and PCB project files.
- `bom/`: bill of materials with quantities, supplier references, estimated/actual costs, and sourcing status.
- `exports/`: reviewed fabrication files, schematic PDFs, and assembly outputs grouped by board revision.

Motor, sensor, driver, power-stage topology, and power supply are not selected yet. Record pin assignments, interfaces, electrical limits, wiring, and commissioning steps when defined. Keep design sources and intentional manufacturing outputs in Git; exclude editor locks and temporary backups.
